Every police unit operates under a strict database-enforced Finite State Machine.

Our fundamental operational breakthrough is: FASTEST DOES NOT EQUAL BEST.

Traditional systems blindly dispatch the closest unit. Nagpur Pulse evaluates:
  1. Traffic-calibrated ETA via TomTom live routing.
  2. Vehicle capability matching (e.g., Heavy PCR Van vs Quick Interceptor).
  3. Officer shift fatigue and active mission workload.
  4. CONSEQUENCE-AWARE SECTOR PRESERVATION: The system simulates whether

Decision Assurance Score (DAS: 0 to 100) and checks 5 Hard Constraint Gates.

hard-blocks the dispatch to prevent operational failure."

================================================================================

[ON SCREEN]: Click on [Risk Intelligence] tab.
[HIGHLIGHT]: 30-D Feature Vector, 4-Class Softmax Probabilities (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH,

[SPOKEN SCRIPT]:
"This is the core AI engine of Nagpur Pulse: Risk Intelligence.

1. MODEL ARCHITECTURE & TRAINING:
   • Our risk classifier is an XGBoost Gradient-Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT).
   • Trained on 2,880 observations across all 44 chowks of Nagpur across 8 time windows.
   • To solve extreme class imbalance (accidents represent <2% of normal traffic time), 
     we trained the model using SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) 
     combined with class-weighted loss penalties.
   • Achieves 99.07% overall accuracy, 100% Critical Recall (zero missed catastrophic 
     incidents), and an ultra-fast 3.9 ms inference latency.

2. EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI VIA SHAP WATERFALL):
   • We never present an AI prediction as a black box. For every intersection, 
     dispatchers see the exact local SHAP feature contributions:
       + Traffic Delay Index (+0.21)
       + Historical 6-Year Accident Density (+0.18)
       + Surface Rainfall Intensity (+0.14)
       - Recent Police Patrol Presence (-0.09)
   • Officers know exactly WHY the AI predicted High Risk, fostering institutional trust."
