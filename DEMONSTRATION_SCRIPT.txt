"This brings us to Stage 2: The Core Machine Learning Risk Engine.

HOW THE MODEL WAS TRAINED:
- Training Dataset: 2,880 observation records across Nagpur's 44 key chowks, 
  partitioned into 1,920 training, 480 validation, and 480 test records.
- Extreme Class Imbalance Handling: Severe emergency crashes naturally represent less 
  than 2% of real-world traffic time. To prevent the model from ignoring rare deadly events, 
  we applied SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) on training splits combined 
  with class-weighted cost functions.
- Selected Architecture: An XGBoost Gradient-Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT) with 30 engineered 
  features, including 7-day, 90-day, and 1-year rolling crash means, lag frequencies, 
  spatial interaction terms, and temporal rush-hour flags.
- Benchmarks: 
  * Overall Model Accuracy: 99.07%
  * Emergency Safety Recall for CRITICAL Crashes: 100.0% (Zero False Negatives)
  * ROC-AUC: 0.9971
  * Inference Latency: 3.9 milliseconds per prediction.

PROBABILISTIC INFERENCE VS BINARY LABELS:
Instead of outputting a naive hardcoded label, our model outputs calibrated Softmax 
probabilities across all 4 tiers:
- LOW Risk (e.g., 3%)
- MEDIUM Risk (e.g., 8%)
- HIGH Risk (e.g., 61%)
- CRITICAL Risk (e.g., 28%)

We then compute a continuous composite Risk Score from 0 to 100 using the formula:
Risk Score = P(Low)*0 + P(Med)*35 + P(High)*70 + P(Crit)*100

This allows our police command center to see smooth, nuanced risk escalation rather than 
sudden jarring alerts."

In Stage 3, Nagpur Pulse implements SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) for real-time 
local feature attribution.

For every single alert, the system answers: 'WHY DID THE MODEL PREDICT THIS RISK?'

As you can see on this waterfall visual:
- Positive risk drivers push the score up towards Critical: 
  * Incident Severity (+0.28)
  * Real-Time Traffic Congestion (+0.21)
  * Historical Fatal Black-Spot Density (+0.18)
  * Surface Rain Intensity (+0.14)
- Mitigating features push the risk down:
  * Recent Police Patrol Presence (-0.09)
  * Physical Median Divider (-0.05)

The dispatcher sees exact mathematical accountability: from the City Baseline Risk of 0.18 
all the way to the final alert output of 0.89."
